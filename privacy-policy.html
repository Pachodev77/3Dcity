Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 4, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to 3D City ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our online multiplayer 3D game.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

  • IP Address: For server connection and geolocation
  • Browser Type: To optimize game performance
  • Device Information: Screen size, operating system
  • Game Data: Avatar selection, position, actions in-game
  • Session Data: Connection time, gameplay duration

2.2 User-Provided Information

  • Chat Messages: Messages sent in the in-game chat
  • Avatar Selection: Your chosen character

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining the game service
  • Multiplayer synchronization via Socket.IO
  • Improving game performance and user experience
  • Displaying relevant advertisements
  • Preventing cheating and abuse

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for game functionality
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with the game
  • Advertising: AdMaven and third-party ad networks use cookies to display relevant ads

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 AdMaven

We use AdMaven to display advertisements. AdMaven may collect and use data as described in their privacy policy: AdMaven Privacy Policy

5.2 Hosting Services

Our game is hosted on Vercel. Data may be processed according to their privacy policy: Vercel Privacy Policy

5.3 Socket.IO

We use Socket.IO for real-time multiplayer communication. Connection data is temporarily stored for session management.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Game data is stored temporarily in server memory
  • No permanent user accounts or passwords are stored
  • Connections use secure WebSocket protocols
  • We implement reasonable security measures to protect data

7. Children's Privacy

Our game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights (GDPR Compliance)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to data processing
  • Data portability

Since we don't store permanent user data, most data is automatically deleted when you close the game.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and request deletion. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
